About this map

This interactive web map shows the extent of moderate drought across the United States for the week of October 13, 2026. Each state is shaded by the percentage of its area in moderate drought (U.S. Drought Monitor category D1), using six classes and a sequential yellow-orange-red color ramp from ColorBrewer. Hovering over a state highlights it and shows its name and drought percentage in an info panel, and clicking a state zooms to it. The map was built with Leaflet, leaflet-ajax, chroma.js, and jQuery, with drought data from the U.S. Drought Monitor (National Drought Mitigation Center, USDA, and NOAA).
